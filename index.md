  <section class="method-section" id="method" aria-labelledby="method-title">
    <div class="section">
      <div class="section-heading reveal">
        <span class="section-number">02 / Contribution method</span>
        <h2 id="method-title">Reality first. Then code.</h2>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="method-grid">
      <article class="method reveal">
        <span class="method-marker">A / Observe</span>
        <h3>Find the actual friction.</h3>
        <p>Start with the stubborn part of the workflow, not the fashionable solution.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="method reveal">
        <span class="method-marker">B / Reduce</span>
        <h3>Build the narrow tool.</h3>
        <p>Cut scope until the useful thing is obvious, testable, and ready to leave the workbench.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="method reveal">
        <span class="method-marker">C / Prove</span>
        <h3>Make the state legible.</h3>
        <p>Good software should show what happened, what failed, and what to do next.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
